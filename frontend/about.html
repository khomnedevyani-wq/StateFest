About StateFest

“One State. Countless Talents. One Platform.”
A statewide event management ecosystem modernizing collegiate participation across Maharashtra.

Project Vision

Bridging Maharashtra's Collegiate Ecosystem

Every year, thousands of college festivals, national hackathons, robotic challenges, and cultural symposiums take place across universities in Maharashtra — from Mumbai and Pune to Nagpur, Sangli, and Aurangabad.

Traditionally, registrations have been fragmented across disparate Google Forms, lost WhatsApp notices, and manual on-spot paperwork. StateFest solves this statewide challenge by providing a centralized, verified, and automated hub for inter-collegiate participation.

Stakeholder Value

Who Benefits from StateFest?

A modern solution tailored for every participant in the higher education circuit.

For Students
  • Unified catalog of statewide hackathons, sports & cultural fests.
  • One-click registration without repeated form filling.
  • Instant verified digital entry passes with unique ID.
  • Realtime capacity and deadline tracking.
For Colleges & Organizers
  • Publish and manage collegiate competitions effortlessly.
  • Automated capacity cutoff and deadline enforcement.
  • One-click CSV exports for spot desk verification.
  • Elimination of duplicate registrations.
For State Directorate / Universities
  • Holistic analytics on collegiate engagement across districts.
  • Standardized event reporting across universities.
  • Transparent student participation records.
  • Promotion of statewide inter-college camaraderie.
